
# AI Usage Log

## AI Tools Used

- ChatGPT by OpenAI.

## Purpose of AI Assistance

AI assistance was used for:
- Understanding the assignment requirements.
- Planning the repository structure.
- Preparing initial documentation templates.
- Learning Git and Python project setup.
- Supporting test design and automation development.

## Prompts and Interactions

The AI was asked to:
- Explain the assignment step by step.
- Recommend a suitable testing framework.
- Help prepare documentation.
- Explain technical commands and errors.

## Human Review

All AI-generated content and code will be reviewed and understood before submission.

## AI Errors and Corrections

To be documented if incorrect suggestions or outputs are identified.

## Rejected Suggestions

To be documented when AI suggestions are rejected, with reasons.

## Work Not Delegated to AI

- Final decisions about test coverage and priorities.
- Validation of actual application behavior.
- Verification of findings and supporting evidence.
- Final review and explanation of the submitted work.

## Reflection

AI was used as an assistant and learning tool, not as a replacement for QA judgment.
